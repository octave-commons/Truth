Why 2 parcels are ejected at startup, with zero input

The observer spawns at the exact center of the nebula (src/domain/genesis.clj:311, position [0 0 0]), with its focus there too, and observer-acceleration-system is live from tick 0 — "reality condenses where you look" is always on, no player action required.

The problem is its magnitude. default-influence-speed in src/domain/player.clj:198 is 1.0e5 m/s, and the comment even says "Tuned to be perceptible." Per tick, each body inside the collapse radius gets:

- Δv = coherence(0.8) × focus-intensity(0.5) × 0.1 × 1e5 = 4,000 m/s per tick
- The cloud's own scale (4e30 kg, 2e16 m) gives virial speed ≈ 115 m/s and escape ≈ 163 m/s

So the nudge is ~25× escape speed every tick. With dt around 1e12 s, one kick moves a parcel ~4e15 m — it crosses the entire 8e14 m collapse zone in a single tick and exits carrying 4 km/s. Instant ejection.

Why exactly two: the collapse radius is focus-radius(1e15) × coherence(0.8) = 8e14 m, only 4% of the cloud radius. Given the seeding distribution (r = R·√u), the expected number of parcels inside that zone at t=0 is ~1–2. Those are your two ejecta.
