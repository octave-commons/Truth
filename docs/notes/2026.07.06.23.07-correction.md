What the note actually says

The two populations form under different physics, and that's the whole point:

- Cores (→ protostars/stars): Jeans instability. Self-gravity beats pressure + turbulence and the gas collapses. This happens at every unstable density maximum — a cloud fragments into many cores (a cluster), wherever gravity wins. Nothing about this is "one." My "single bootstrap" idea was backwards.
- Planetesimals: solids particulating out of the gas. As the note puts it (and HOPS-315 shows): gas cools below the silicate/SiO condensation temperature, refractory solids condense out, the dust-to-gas ratio climbs, and past a streaming-instability threshold the solids self-concentrate and collapse into planetesimals — without the gas collapsing globally. It's a thermal + dust-concentration trigger on the solid budget, not a gravitational-collapse trigger on the gas.

So they're orthogonal. A parcel can be Jeans-unstable (→ a gas core) or cool-with-concentrated-dust (→ precipitates planetesimal super-particles). Two triggers, two size regimes: gas collapse gives you the parcel-mass-and-up bodies (stars, giants), solid precipitation gives you the small 1e16 bodies. That's exactly why your old "collapse whole parcels" model couldn't make anything smaller than 1-4k Earth masses — it only had the gas channel.

Where the current code is wrong

classify-next-state's :nebula branch (stellar.clj:1024) uses one trigger — Jeans + density — and then splits on mass: sub-opacity → :planetesimal, super → core. That's the mis-gating. It labels a Jeans-unstable gas collapse as a "planetesimal" whenever the parcel is light. At production resolution every parcel is light, so the dense, gravitationally-collapsing center gets mislabeled and shunted into seed-and-grow → your central planetesimal swarm, while a lone clump squeaks through as the core. The planetesimal channel is firing on the core's trigger.

The corrected fix

1. Jeans-unstable + dense → :condensed-core, regardless of mass. Drop the mass-based → :planetesimal from that branch. Many form, at every unstable maximum (isolation still stops wholesale condensation). They accrete/merge up the ladder to star/giant/brown-dwarf. → dense center now makes a core, and you get a cluster, not one hardcoded star.
2. Re-gate condensation-seeder-system on precipitation, not Jeans — trigger on parcel temperature below the silicate/metal condensation range + solid (metals) concentration, spawning planetesimal super-particles from the solid mass budget. Decoupled from the gas-collapse trigger entirely.
