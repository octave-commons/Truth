# 2026.07.05 — Player influence as a dark halo (and live Spark knobs)

## The bug

Two gas parcels were ejected within seconds of a fresh dev sim, with zero
player input; moving the mote or placing wells ejected more. Root causes:

- The observer's pull-toward-focus nudge (`player/default-influence-speed`
  1e5 m/s) delivered Δv ≈ coherence·intensity·0.1·1e5 = **4,000 m/s per tick**
  to every body inside the collapse radius — ~25× the cloud's escape speed
  (~163 m/s) — and it is active from tick 0, with the observer (and its focus)
  spawned at the origin. The ~1–2 parcels seeded inside the 8e14 m collapse
  radius were flung out in one or two ticks. Moving the mote re-pins the focus
  to it, sweeping the ejection bubble through the cloud. This is the same bug
  the warp well had already fixed for itself (2e4 → 1.5e2 m/s, "sized to the
  cloud") — the observer nudge was never resized.
- The well's virial-scale kick was still an **additive Δv with no cumulative
  bound**: ~45 km/s of total budget over its 600-tick ttl, so anything that
  lingered near the core crossed escape speed and slingshotted out.

## The model

Both influences are now the same field: a **Plummer halo** — the player as a
large, diffuse body of mass ("dark matter you can steer"), not a point kick.
`law.stellar/plummer-acceleration`: g(r) = G·M·r/(r²+a²)^{3/2} — zero at the
centre, peak at r = a/√2, Keplerian far field; the identical field family
`softened-circular-speed` orbits (v_c²/r = g, pinned by test).

Why this shape solves it structurally rather than by tuning:

- A **static** halo is a conservative field: matter gains exactly the speed
  falling in that it loses climbing out. It deepens the local potential well —
  binds and gathers — and cannot pump a body past escape speed.
- Ejection remains possible but requires **work**: dragging or re-narrowing a
  strong halo. That is the deliberate-destruction seam — when destructive play
  should cost coherence, drain it on ejection events; halo mass ∝ coherence
  then makes the ability self-limiting.

Scaling:

- Observer halo mass = `:genesis/observer-halo-mass-factor` (default 2.0) ×
  coherence × focus-intensity × seeded cloud mass. Scale radius = focus-radius
  (default raised 1e15 → 5e15 m; `,`/`.` narrow/widen concentrate the same
  mass). Reach = 3 scale radii (`player/halo-reach-factor`).
- Well/repulsor mass = `:genesis/well-mass-factor` (default 0.5) × strength ×
  decay × cloud mass, scale radius = its `:radius`, sign flipped for repulsors.
- dt backstop, NOT the design lever: per-tick |Δv| capped at
  `:genesis/influence-dv-cap` (default 1.0) × cloud virial speed
  (`player/influence-reference`; warp caps the summed field).

## Live knobs (Spark panel)

All influence magic numbers are now world keys, adjustable at runtime from the
Spark menu panel (`infra.menu/spark-knobs` — same stepper rows as View):
focus radius/intensity, halo mass factor, Δv cap, well mass/radius/ttl, heat
approach rate. Defaults are seeded by `genesis/create-world`. Knob clicks are
sim-side actions: `menu/world-action` returns a world→world' fn the window
loop enqueues on the intent queue, so they land between ticks like any input
(never a render-thread world write).

## Verified

Fresh pm2 run: 0 of 988 parcels beyond 1.5R after 1.5k ticks (was: 2 ejecta
immediately). A max-strength well placed at the origin through the intent
queue lived its full ttl: still 0 ejecta, cloud max radius shrank, 40 parcels
gathered inside the well radius. Sim healthy at 9.5k ticks (star formed,
accretion phase).
