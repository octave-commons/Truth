# Authentic Phase 0 — The Physics of Forming a Solar System

**Status:** physics design spec for Phase 0 formation. Drives the single-writer
classifier and the collapse/disk systems. Companion to the double-buffer spec.
**Date:** 2026-06-26
**Mandate:** maximum achievable accuracy. Every beat grounded in the real physics
of how our Sun and its planets formed, until time locks and the Gates of Truth
are invented. No creative liberty before that point — only the honest limits of
what the engine's resolution can resolve.

---

## 0. Where we start, and what we assume

We open **mid-life of the universe**, not at the Big Bang. This single framing
choice resolves the "where do the metals come from?" question without us
simulating any nucleosynthesis:

- The nebula is **pre-enriched, Population I** gas — the ashes of earlier stellar
  generations. The most massive stars live only a few Myr and die as supernovae;
  over billions of years they have seeded the interstellar medium with C, N, O,
  Si, Fe and the rest. Those metals are an **initial condition**, not something
  we make.
- Proto-solar composition by mass: **X ≈ 0.71 (H), Y ≈ 0.27 (He), Z ≈ 0.015
  (metals)**. The metals matter enormously later: **Z is the dust — the raw
  material every planet is built from.**
- We do **not** model fusion nucleosynthesis, r/s-process, or supernova
  enrichment. The story is *gravitational and thermodynamic*, on gas whose
  chemistry was written by stars we never see.

This is authentic: it is exactly the proto-solar nebula a real simulation of our
Sun's birth would initialize.

## 1. The honest resolution limit (read this first)

The cloud is a few M_⊙ sampled by ~10³ equal-mass gas parcels. So one parcel ≈
M_⊙/250 ≈ **~4 M_Jupiter**. This sets a hard ceiling on what is *resolvable*:

- **Stars and brown dwarfs ARE resolvable.** A 0.08 M_⊙ star is ~20 parcels
  falling together; a brown dwarf is a few. The whole gas→core→protostar→star
  track is honest N-body + thermodynamics.
- **Planets are NOT resolvable from the gas.** Real planet formation happens
  10+ orders of magnitude in mass *below* a parcel: dust → mm pebbles → km
  planetesimals → protoplanets → planets. You cannot grow Earth by merging
  4-M_Jup gas blobs; that would be a lie dressed as emergence.

**This is not a failure of the engine — it is how real planet-formation science
works.** Hydro simulations resolve the collapsing cloud, the protostar, and the
disk; **planet formation is a sub-grid model** (core accretion / population
synthesis) layered on the disk's surface density, run separately. So Phase 0 has
**three tracks**, each modeled at its honest fidelity:

| Track | Fidelity | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Gas → star** | resolved N-body + thermo | gravity, Jeans, collapse, fusion |
| **Disk** | resolved (rotational flattening) | angular-momentum support of infall |
| **Planets** | **sub-grid** prescription | core accretion on disk solid/gas surface density |

Pretending planets emerge from parcel merging is the *less* authentic choice. The
sub-grid disk model is the accurate one.

## 2. The formation beats (the documentary)

Each transition is *forced* by physics — that is the source of the awe. Real
thresholds, with the engine mapping.

### Beat 1 — Molecular cloud (`:nebula`)
Cold (**10–20 K**), `n ~ 10²–10⁴ cm⁻³` → **ρ ~ 10⁻¹⁸–10⁻¹⁶ kg/m³**, mostly H₂.
Held up against gravity by thermal + turbulent + magnetic pressure. Rotating.
*Engine:* already seeded — Pop I parcels, sub-virial rotation, turbulence,
per-body B. ✓

### Beat 2 — Jeans collapse (the trigger)
A bound region collapses when its mass exceeds the **Jeans mass**
`M_J ∝ c_s³ / √(G³ρ) ∝ T^{3/2} ρ^{-1/2}`. Below M_J, pressure wins and the clump
oscillates; above it, gravity wins irreversibly.
*Engine:* gravity (Barnes–Hut) already pulls parcels into filaments and knots.
The Jeans criterion identifies **which** overdense regions are unstable. Collapse
is **collective** — many parcels falling together — not a per-parcel event. A
parcel is "in collapse" when it sits in a region where enclosed mass > M_J.

### Beat 3 — Isothermal collapse → first core
Free-fall inward; the gas stays ~isothermal because it radiates heat away while
density runs up (`ρ: 10⁻¹⁶ → 10⁻¹⁰ kg/m³`). When the center becomes **optically
thick (~10⁻¹⁰ kg/m³)** heat can no longer escape: it switches to **adiabatic**
(`T ∝ ρ^{2/3}` for monatomic γ=5/3) and a pressure-supported **first hydrostatic
core** forms. At **~2000 K** H₂ dissociates (endothermic), support fails, and a
second collapse forms a true stellar-density core.
*Engine:* the **density threshold** (`ρ ≳ 10⁻¹⁰ kg/m³` central) is the authentic
`:nebula → :protostar` gate — a *density/temperature* condition, **not** a mass
tier. The body has become a self-gravitating hydrostatic object.

### Beat 4 — Protostar + disk (`:protostar`)
The core accretes the infalling envelope. **Angular momentum cannot be destroyed**
→ infall flattens into a **rotationally-supported accretion disk**; excess
material and field launch **bipolar jets**. The protostar contracts on the
**Kelvin–Helmholtz timescale** (~10–100 Myr), `T_core` climbing as gravitational
energy converts to heat, bounded below by the **virial temperature**
`T_vir ≈ G M m_H / (k_B R)`.
*Engine:* `collapse-system` already does rate-limited KH contraction, adiabatic
heating, virial floor, flux-freezing, and oblateness from L. The **disk** is the
flattened (high-oblateness) rotationally-supported envelope — and the substrate
for the planet sub-grid.

### Beat 5 — Ignition (`:star`) — and the failures
- **Deuterium burning at ~10⁶ K** marks a true protostar (halts contraction
  briefly; the T-Tauri glow).
- **Sustained hydrogen fusion at ~10⁷ K** requires **M ≥ 0.08 M_⊙ (~1.6×10²⁹
  kg)** — radiation + thermal pressure reach hydrostatic equilibrium. A **star**,
  on the main sequence.
- **Brown dwarf (`:brown-dwarf`):** **0.013–0.08 M_⊙ (~2.5×10²⁸–1.6×10²⁹ kg)** —
  burns deuterium, contraction halted by **electron degeneracy** before H ignites.
  Glows and fades. A real, distinct beat.
*Engine:* `fusion-system` tests T/P/H. Authentic refinement: gate the star
transition on **mass ≥ 0.08 M_⊙ AND T ≥ 10⁷**; route 0.013–0.08 M_⊙ cores whose
contraction stalls to `:brown-dwarf`.

### Beat 6 — Planets (sub-grid, in the disk)
Within the disk, over Myr:
- **Dust (the metals, Z) settles** to the midplane and **coagulates** into
  planetesimals; the **snow line** (where T drops below ~150–170 K so water ice
  condenses) sharply raises the solid surface density beyond it.
- **Core accretion:** planetesimals → protoplanets. Inside the snow line, rock/
  metal → **terrestrial** planets (slow, giant impacts, ~10⁸ yr). Beyond it, a
  **~10 M_⊕ core** forms fast enough to **runaway-accrete H/He** before the gas
  disperses (~3–10 Myr) → **gas giants**. Ice giants: cores that grabbed only a
  little gas before the disk cleared.
- **Disk clearing:** stellar radiation + winds + photoevaporation remove the gas;
  orbits settle; leftover planetesimals are the **debris** belt.
*Engine:* a sub-grid model keyed on the disk's local solid/gas surface density,
orbital period, and the snow-line temperature — seeding `:planet` (sub-typed
`:terrestrial`/`:gas-giant`/`:ice-giant`) and `:debris` as disk bodies, **not**
from gas-parcel merges. Mass below ~13 M_Jup ⇒ planet, not brown dwarf.

## 3. The authentic state machine (the classifier)

One classifier (`stellar/classifier-system`, sole writer of `matter-state`) owns
the transitions. The two physical axes are **separated**: *collapse* (Jeans +
density) decides **whether** something condenses; *mass + ignition* decide **what
it becomes**. The classifier reads each body's physics from the frozen snapshot
and evaluates the conditions **inline** — there are no separate signal components
(an earlier draft proposed them; computing inline proved simpler).

```
:nebula ──[ Jeans-unstable AND (ρ ≥ ρ_core ~1e-10  OR  accreted past 1 parcel) ]──┐
            then by mass:  M ≥ 0.013 M_⊙ → :protostar   else → :debris            │
:debris ──[ accreted to M ≥ 0.013 M_⊙ ]───────────────────────────▶ :protostar   │
:protostar ──[ T ≥ 1e7 K AND M ≥ 0.08 M_⊙ AND X_H>0.1 ]───────────▶ :star        │
:protostar ──[ contraction stalled, 0.013 ≤ M < 0.08 M_⊙ ]────────▶ :brown-dwarf │
(disk sub-grid, beat 6) ──[ M < 13 M_Jup ]────────────────────────▶ :planet      │
:star, :brown-dwarf, :planet are terminal in the classifier.  ────────────────────┘
```

The conditions, all evaluated inline by the classifier:

| Condition | Predicate | Decides |
|---|---|---|
| Jeans-unstable | `stellar/jeans-unstable?` (region radius > Jeans length) | condensation |
| core density | ρ ≥ `stellar/core-condensation-density` (1e-10) | condensation |
| ignition | `law/fusion-possible?` (T≥1e7 ∧ P ∧ X_H>0.1) ∧ M ≥ `hydrogen-burning-mass` | → star |
| stalled | `stellar/contraction-stalled?` (radius at floor, T<1e7) ∧ M < `hydrogen-burning-mass` | → brown dwarf |

Mass sets *fate*, density+Jeans set *condensation*, temperature sets *ignition* —
never one standing in for another. This is both the authentic causality and the
single-writer resolution: `jeans`, `fusion`, `collapse`, `classify` stop writing
`matter-state` and raise flags; the classifier is its sole writer.

## 4. Calibrating the toy scale honestly

The cloud is toy-scaled (a few M_⊙, ~10³ parcels), but the **thresholds and
relationships are real**, expressed in SI so they carry physical meaning:

- Densities: cloud `1e-16`, core `1e-10`, stellar `1e3` kg/m³ — the *real* ladder.
- Temperatures: cloud `~15`, H₂-dissociation `~2000`, deuterium `~1e6`, H-burning
  `~1e7` K — real.
- Masses (real boundaries, not the current toy tiers): H-burning `0.08 M_⊙`,
  brown-dwarf floor `0.013 M_⊙`, planet ceiling `13 M_Jup`.
- KH time, Jeans mass, virial T, ideal-gas EOS, `T ∝ ρ^{2/3}` adiabat,
  flux-freezing `B ∝ ρ^{2/3}` — all already real laws in `domain.stellar` /
  `law.stellar` / `law.field`.

What stays "toy" is only the **number of particles and the cloud's absolute
mass** — i.e. the *resolution*, not the *physics*. That is the same compromise
every real simulation makes.

## 5. Build order

1. **Classifier (single-writer `matter-state`)** — implement the §3 state machine
   on the *resolved* track (nebula→protostar→star/brown-dwarf). Density-gated
   condensation, mass+ignition for fate. Retire mass-tier promotion as the
   condensation trigger. (This is double-buffer migration step 6.)
2. **Collapse/structure authenticity** — isothermal→adiabatic switch at the
   optically-thick density; degeneracy floor for brown dwarfs.
3. **Disk identification** — derive the rotationally-supported disk region from
   oblateness + angular momentum around a protostar/star.
4. **Planet sub-grid** — core-accretion prescription on disk solid/gas surface
   density with a snow line; seeds `:planet`/`:debris`. The least-resolved track,
   modeled the way the science models it.

Beats 1–5 are achievable at full accuracy now. Beat 6 (planets) is the sub-grid
frontier — honest about being a prescription, faithful to the real mechanism.

**Fixed resolution for all of the above.** Adaptive resolution (§6) is an
optimization and is explicitly deferred: we make the physics *correct* from
nebula to planets first, because optimizing an inaccurate system locks in bad
decisions.

## 6. Deferred — adaptive multi-resolution (the substrate as many models, one fabric)

> **Status: documented intent, explicitly DEFERRED.** Do not build until the
> fixed-resolution formation (beats 1–5 + the planet sub-grid) is both physically
> correct and runs with acceptable fundamental algorithmic performance.

The mature substrate runs **several physical models at once inside the one ECS
world** — diffuse gas-hydro, the dense collapse / stellar-interior model, the
solids / core-accretion microphysics — each authoritative over matter in a
different regime. They **overlap in time**: the star is still accreting gas while
the disk is forming while solids are already clumping in it.

In that picture the **classifier is the broker between models.** Its role grows
from "set `matter-state`" to "decide when **mass and energy hand off** from one
model's authority to another" — a gas parcel's core crossing into the
stellar-interior model, disk gas captured into a forming planet's envelope,
leftover solids dropping into the debris model. **`matter-state` is the ledger of
which model currently owns each entity** — and therefore which systems are
allowed to act on it. (Even at *fixed* resolution this broker role is real: it is
how an entity stops being governed by gas-hydro and starts being governed by the
collapse model. Only the *resolution-scaling* below is deferred.)

The player's extension: **the magnitude of mass/energy under a model's influence
in a region drives the resolution that model runs at there.** A region holding a
lot of actively-collapsing material earns finer parcels and faster timesteps; a
quiescent diffuse region coarsens. Resolution becomes an adaptive per-region
field scaled by *significance* — the spatial analog of the complexity-scaled
**time** clock already in place. Scale time *and* scale space, both by where the
story is happening.

Because the physics is **scale-free**, this stays ONE substrate (the single-ECS
principle holds): not parallel sims, but the same double-buffer systems over
entities of varying scale, realized by **particle splitting/merging** and
**block / individual timesteps** (the zoom-in / AMR family). The scale-free
single-writer systems of the double-buffer spec are exactly what make
heterogeneous-resolution entities tractable in one tick. Adaptive refinement
later *renegotiates* the resolved↔sub-grid boundary of §1; it never erases it.

## 7. Body structure & collisions — ellipsoid now, voxels for the large stable bodies (deferred)

A body's geometry is currently an **ellipsoid**: `radius` + `oblateness` +
`rotation-axis` — a rotating, flattened sphere. That is *correct* for fluid
bodies (nebula clumps, protostars, stars) — they really are self-gravitating
fluid ellipsoids. But once the star stabilizes and solid planets **clear their
orbits**, the bodies become rigid, layered objects and the interesting physics
becomes **collisions** — impacts that crater, deform, shatter, and eject. A
point-overlap merge ("poof") cannot tell that story.

So **`geometry` is the seam where the shape representation deepens with a body's
significance** — the solid-body twin of the gas adaptive-refinement (§6):

- **Fluid bodies:** ellipsoid (radius, oblateness, axis). Cheap and correct.
- **Large stable solid bodies (planets, major moons):** a **voxel volume** — a
  3-D grid of material cells (rock / metal / ice / volatiles), giving the body
  real internal structure and a real surface.

With voxels, **collisions become geometric**: an impactor penetrates, deposits
momentum and heat locally, excavates a crater, shatters cells into `:debris`
ejecta, and at high energy disrupts the body. That is the authentic form of the
late-stage story — the Moon-forming giant impact, late heavy bombardment,
differentiation — replacing the literal-overlap merge. It is also exactly the
**classifier-as-broker** (§6) operating at solid-body resolution: an impact hands
mass between the body model and the debris model, voxel by voxel.

**Deferred, like adaptive refinement.** We design `geometry` *now* as an abstract
**shape representation** (an ellipsoid today) behind which a voxel volume can
later live, for bodies the simulation deems large and stable enough to earn the
cost — resolution-by-significance again (a focused planet earns voxels; a distant
pebble stays a point). Fixed ellipsoid representation now; voxel collisions are a
later beat, after the formation sequence is correct end-to-end. **The design
constraint this imposes today:** the structural owner (§ below) must treat shape
as a value it *owns and could swap*, not as three loose scalar components other
systems reach into — so the ellipsoid→voxel upgrade is a change of one owner, not
a cross-cutting rewrite.

### 7b. Untangling collapse — the structural owners (AS BUILT)

`collapse-system` transformed a protostar's geometry *and* thermodynamics in one
coupled adiabatic step, co-writing `radius`, `density`, `temperature`, `spin`,
`b-field`. Physically these are **not five independent quantities** — they are all
driven by one process: the **contraction of the body's structure**. So the clean,
authentic decomposition makes structure primary and the rest *derive* from it:

| Quantity | Owner | Relationship (as built) |
|---|---|---|
| `radius`, `density`, `oblateness`, `rotation-axis` | **Structure** (`stellar/structure-system`) | one geometric fact; radius & density owned together (gas → SPH; solid → material density; core → KH oblate contraction). The future voxel home. |
| `spin` | **EM** (`domain.em`) | *derived* ω = L / I(mass, radius) — spin-up emerges as I shrinks; no torque accumulator |
| `temperature` | **Thermal** (`stellar/temperature-system`) | core: **virial** T = G M m_H/(k_B R), a pure derivation from mass+radius (no compression-ΔT accumulator); solid: radiative; nebula: untouched |
| `b-field` | **Field** (`em/field-system`) | conserved **frozen flux** Φ = B·R² ⇒ B = Φ/R² (amplifies as R contracts) + Ohmic decay |

Structure drives; everything else is a function of (shape, density, L). This is
both the authentic causal order (contraction is the cause; heating / spin-up /
flux-amplification are effects) and the single-writer resolution. `collapse` and
`density-system` are fully **retired** from the fan-out.

> What changed from the first draft of this section: `density` is owned by
> **Structure** (paired with radius — they are one geometric fact), not a separate
> Density owner; `spin`/`temperature`/`b-field` became **derivations**, not
> integrators fed by `torque`/`heat`/`dB/dt` contributions. The derivations are
> simpler and exact. See the double-buffer spec §4.

### 7c. Go-live status — the accretion-vs-condensation gap (CLOSED)

**The parallel single-writer path is now the live default** (`create-world` sets
`:phase0/parallel? true`). It forms a star — at the test resolution and at the
full ~10³-parcel production cloud — where the legacy sequential pipeline forms
*nothing at all*. Closing the gap took three fixes, all in the *content*, not the
substrate:

1. **The feeding-zone race (the actual blocker).** A condensed body assembles a
   core only by literal-overlap merging, and a body is collidable only if it
   carries an `accretion-radius` (its gravitational feeding zone). That zone was
   owned by the legacy `jeans-collapse` system, which gated on a *different*
   predicate than the classifier — `radius > λ_J` with the γ-corrected Jeans
   length, vs the classifier's `jeans-unstable?` (γ-free) **and** density gate. So
   the classifier condensed parcels one tick *before* `jeans-collapse` would have
   fired; the next tick they were already `:protostar`/`:debris` and
   `jeans-collapse` (which only touches `:nebula`) skipped them forever. **Every
   condensed body had no feeding zone → was never collidable → never merged → no
   star** (the swarm of individually-condensed sub-stellar bodies the earlier
   draft of this section predicted). Fix: `stellar/accretion-zone-system` — a
   native write-set owner of `accretion-radius` that reuses the classifier's *own*
   `classify-next-state` decision, latching the feeding zone on the **same frozen
   tick** a parcel condenses, captured from the wide *gas* smoothing radius before
   Structure's KH contraction shrinks the photosphere.

2. **Feeding-zone scale (`stellar/feeding-zone-factor`, resolution-aware).** The
   zone must span ~2× the initial inter-parcel spacing (≈ extent/N^(1/3)) so the
   first overdense body reaches *several* neighbours and runs away. At the
   condensation smoothing length that is a factor of a few hundred (floor 600,
   raised for coarser clouds by `resolution-feeding-zone-factor`). With the old
   `50×` the production cloud fragmented into ~550 sub-stellar cores (largest
   ~0.03 M⊙) instead of assembling one; at the floor it ignites by ~t=180.

3. **A latent parallel-only NPE.** `entity->region` omitted `:position`, but the
   parallel `temperature-system` reads `(:position region)` to compute stellar
   irradiation of `:debris`/`:planet`. It never crashed before because the
   parallel path had never produced debris *and* a star together; once it did,
   `sp/dist nil …` threw. Fix: `entity->region` now carries `:position`.

(Two earlier bugs, already noted: a `mass > gas-particle-mass` condensation gate
that deadlocked, replaced by the density gate `stellar/core-condensation-density`;
and the SPH density runaway it exposed, now capped by condensation.)

**Why parallel beats sequential as the default.** The legacy pipeline only ever
condensed when a parcel's mass crossed a `law/mass-class` tier — which never
happens for a single equal-mass parcel — so it forms a star *only* at the
coincidental resolutions where one parcel already exceeds a tier (e.g. the gc50
test). At the production kilo-parcel cloud it produces a static, perpetual
nebula. The density-gated parallel path is both the authentic physics and the one
that actually forms solar systems across resolutions. The sequential pipeline is
retained behind `:phase0/parallel? false` for comparison and tests.
