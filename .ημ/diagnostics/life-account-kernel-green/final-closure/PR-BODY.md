The represented-life design needs a conservative account boundary before any ECS producer can create a cohort. This PR implements that first admitted three-point child: named Malli laws and a pure JVM kernel that converts supplied binary64 material to an exact integer carbon budget, applies origin/extent/closure operations, and returns immutable outcomes for the later caller to retain.

`domain.life-account/origin-budget` uses the supplied input bits, exact rational multiplication and one floor into 10^-15 kg-C units. It validates the composition sum without renormalizing it and preserves the specified bounded allocation. `apply-operation` enforces stock conservation and revision rules, distinguishes exact retry from conflicting reuse, and keeps historical outcomes separate from the current account. Raw floating-point bits are compared recursively in map keys, values and unordered collections. Supplied accepted history cannot contradict the current account through a missing/older account, reopened closure or changed origin material; this does not claim globally complete history.

The earlier represented-life proposal and research remain in the PR as grounding. Only `life-account-origin-kernel` is implemented and canonically in Review. The parent specification remains unfinished. The kernel does not create an entity, persist a ledger, identify a natural habitat, generate a timed biological action, or connect to ECS/lifecycle, input/rendering, paid Grow, an avatar or a Gate. Allocation parameters remain the documented tunable model prescription, not empirical calibration. JVM `.clj` is explicit because exact BigInteger/Ratio and binary64 behavior are required; cross-host equivalence is not claimed.
