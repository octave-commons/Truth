Malli laws and a pure JVM domain namespace, with two corresponding test files.

binary64-to-rational carbon inventory and the prescribed integer partition;
`apply-operation` decides origin, caller-supplied extent and terminal closure.

local consistency only, not complete historical truth or physical eligibility.

increments, immutable rejection retention, retry/conflict separation and closed
account history. Raw floating-point identity applies recursively to map keys,
values and unordered members without losing multiplicity. JVM `.clj` is explicit:
existing composition contracts and BigInteger/Ratio/binary64 semantics are used,
with no unsupported portable-host claim.
