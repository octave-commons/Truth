## Why

As a Truth researcher, I need geology atmosphere impacts and chemistry to produce finite physical habitats, and life to modify those habitats, so we can test the emergence and environmental effects of life in one causal voxel world.

## Outcome and acceptance

Run matched cooling-world, hydrothermal and impact/recovery scenarios with conserved material/energy exchange, reference checks and falsifiable biological feedback. Start with the actual current-model probes, not a claim that these coupled scenarios already run. [Design](../../docs/designs/physical-habitat-coupling.md); [research](../../docs/research/planetary-habitats/README.md).
