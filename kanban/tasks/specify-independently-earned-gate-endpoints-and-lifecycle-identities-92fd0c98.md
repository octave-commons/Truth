## Context

The original user requires both worlds to have learned Gate technology and a
receiving device to exist before travel. Separate attributable histories are
proposed below as the meaning of independent earning. Current code has
discovery reward/notification consumers but no Gate producer or endpoint
identity contract. This three-point design child makes that prerequisite precise
without prescribing the missing civilization, construction or travel laws.
Parent: `embodied-character-voxel-mode`. Evidence owner:
`a2b46a04-d541-4714-aed9-bf07d9a9c923`.

## Outcome

A reviewed endpoint identity/provenance contract that distinguishes historical
technology and construction evidence from operability, connection, activation
and traversal. The linked design is a proposal for review, not executable law.

## Scope

Define semantic references for the participating planetary world/history,
civilization and device; retain causal construction and technology evidence in
the existing event model. Separate those references from a connection, activation
attempt and embodied traveler. Document producer/consumer responsibilities,
unavailable producers and required evidence. Work through the bounded cases in
the design and preserve every unresolved policy explicitly.

Coordinate semantics with `life-to-represented-actor-spec` and
`committed-clock-executable-policy`. They are independent planning references,
not fabricated blocking dependencies. Implementation will require the missing
actor/civilization/research/construction and Gate-policy contracts; this card
does not admit their implementation or break the epic into executable stories.

## Non-goals

No Gate spawning, fixture destination, generated avatar, second world model,
network implementation, numerical technology threshold, build recipe, energy
formula, debit/refund policy, operability rule, clock change or native interaction.
Do not turn the six-threshold UI string or discovery rewards into admission or
activation costs. No Ready/In Progress transition is part of this draft.

## Acceptance criteria

- Original user requirements, adopted design direction, assistant sketches,
  inspected source and proposed contract choices remain distinguishable.
- References disambiguate local ECS IDs across histories and distinguish a
  planetary world from its containing ECS store; allocation authority remains
  explicit where the existing source supplies none.
- A constructed receiver requires prior attributable technology/construction
  evidence from its own world's history. Neither an attempted visit, UI name,
  reward event nor existence of a civilization alone supplies that evidence.
- Worked cases cover two attested endpoints, missing receiver, technology without
  construction, equal local IDs in distinct histories, replayed evidence,
  destroyed device and vanished civilization. Historical attestation never
  silently becomes present operability or traversability.
- Creation/discovery clock conflict, pre-resolved destination history, energy and
  construction prices, receiver operability and embodiment ordering remain open
  with named coordination owners or explicitly unassigned future responsibilities.
- Independent review checks the narrow contract against the cited corpus and
  current event/identity owners. Any broader policy needed to complete it is
  recorded separately rather than invented or absorbed into this estimate.

## Verification

Check document links and pinned source objects; inspect canonical Rheos identity,
parent and Incoming state. Review the reference table and worked traces for
provenance loss, duplicate evidence and accidental admission. No disposable
example, unit fixture or design approval counts as a natural Gate-producing run.

## Risks

The same word "world" names a planet, an independent history and an ECS map.
Historical construction can be mistaken for a currently functioning receiver.
Opaque identifiers can hide missing producers. Keep these distinctions visible;
no executable schema or producer is delivered by this planning task.
